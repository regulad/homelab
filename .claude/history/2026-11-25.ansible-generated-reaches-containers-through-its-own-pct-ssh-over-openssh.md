# ansible-generated reaches containers through its own pct_ssh, over OpenSSH

- community.proxmox's `proxmox_pct_remote` connects with paramiko, and Ansible runs each task in a worker of its own, so every task opened and authenticated a new connection to the node (ansible-collections/community.proxmox#550, open). burgerga's drop-in from that issue, gist `a6d784eb` at revision `5ef0fec7`, subclasses `ansible.builtin.ssh`'s connection and wraps each command in `pct exec <vmid> --`; adopted as `ansible-generated/plugins/connection/pct_ssh.py`, it was the only part of community.proxmox homelab used, so the collection's pin went with it.
- Its control path hashes the node's host, port and user, so every container on prodingus shares one master: a check of tsexit and uptime ran its ten SSH commands through the master an earlier run had opened (one `ControlPath`, the master's pid unchanged), with no new authentication. The containers' `HostKeyAlias` is their `ansible_host`, the node's tailnet name, under which `ansible-generated/known_hosts` now pins the node's key, copied from `ansible-hardware/known_hosts`.
- The same tagged check of tsexit took 17 s on `proxmox_pct_remote` and 12 s on `pct_ssh`, with facts and inventory cached.
