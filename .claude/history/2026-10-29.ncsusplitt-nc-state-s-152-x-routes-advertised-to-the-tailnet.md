# ncsusplitt: NC State's 152.x routes advertised to the tailnet (2026-10-29)

- Given subnets, vpn-slice routes only those through the tunnel, plus /32s for the VPN's DNS servers (`152.1.14.12`, `152.1.14.14`). The other split-include routes NC State pushes, `10.0.0.0/8` and the `172.x` ranges in the tailnet policy's `ipset:ncstate--net`, are not routed unless vpn-slice gets `-S`. NC State's internal names resolve to `10.x` addresses (ncsudrive to `10.36.3.56` and `.57`).
- ncsusplitt had `net.ipv4.ip_forward = 1` with no sysctl file setting it, and IPv6 forwarding off. Its eth0 takes IPv6 by RA through systemd-networkd (the kernel's `accept_ra` is 0 there), and networkd turns `IPv6AcceptRA=` off by default when IPv6 forwarding is on, globally included, so `kernel_params_ip_forwarding` would cost such a container its IPv6.
