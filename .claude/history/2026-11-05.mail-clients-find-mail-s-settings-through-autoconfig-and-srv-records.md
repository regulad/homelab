# Mail clients find mail's settings through autoconfig and SRV records (2026-11-05)

- Thunderbird and Nextcloud Mail use Mozilla's autoconfig format: they fetch `https://autoconfig.<domain>/mail/config-v1.1.xml?emailaddress=<address>`, then `https://<domain>/.well-known/autoconfig/mail/config-v1.1.xml`, then Mozilla's provider database, then guess server names. Thunderbird does not use RFC 6186's SRV records. An `autoconfig` Worker serves the file at `autoconfig.` in regulad.xyz, nefumllc.com and regulad.net, listing the three domains and mail's IMAP over TLS on 993 and submission over TLS on 465, or with STARTTLS on 587. Each zone also publishes `_imaps._tcp`, `_submissions._tcp` and `_submission._tcp` SRV records for clients that use them.
- The file has clients log in with the email address, `%EMAILADDRESS%`. Dovecot accepts only `<uid>@regulad.xyz`, so an account set up with an alternate address, such as `pw@nefumllc.com`, needs its login changed to `regulad@regulad.xyz` by hand, until Dovecot's lookup accepts any of a user's addresses.
- Gmail publishes no discovery mechanism. Google is removing POP fetching ("Check mail from other accounts"), Gmailify, and "Send mail as" for third-party addresses: none take new setups after the first quarter of 2026, and all go in January 2027. The Gmail apps for Android and iOS keep IMAP accounts, entered by hand.
- regulad.xyz's `autoconfig` CNAME and its `autodiscover` CNAME and `_autodiscover._tcp` SRV, for Outlook, pointed at mail, where nothing serves HTTPS. The `autoconfig` CNAME is gone: a Worker's custom domain cannot share a name with another DNS record, and OpenTofu cannot order deleting one resource before creating an unrelated one, so a plan targeted at the CNAME deleted it before the plan that created the custom domains. The new host answered 500 for under a minute while Cloudflare provisioned it. The Outlook records remain.
- alock applies a saved plan only when its file is named `<anything>@<commit>.tfplan`, the commit last: `cloudflare@<commit>-1.tfplan` was refused, and `cloudflare-1@<commit>.tfplan` ran.
- In a `<<-` heredoc, template directives with strip markers (`%{for …~}`) shifted the indentation of the lines around them; the `<domain>` lines are built with `join()` and interpolated on a line of their own instead. The zones mail receives for, which both Workers serve, are one map, `local.mail_zones`, in `locals.tf`.
