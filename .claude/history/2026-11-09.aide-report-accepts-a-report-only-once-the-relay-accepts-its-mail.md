# aide-report accepts a report only once the relay accepts its mail (2026-11-09)

- **A queued mail is not a delivered one.** aide-report handed its mail to mail(1), which returns once Postfix has queued it, and accepted the reported files at once. While the relay refused freeipa's logins (from 10-27), its reports were accepted with their mails still in the queue, and one expired undelivered: its changes were never seen.
- aide-report now gives the mail to Postfix's `sendmail -i root` with a Message-ID of its own, then reads Postfix's log in the journal (`SYSLOG_IDENTIFIER` starting `postfix/`, from a second before the send, as journalctl reads whole seconds) every 5 seconds for up to 5 minutes. cleanup's `<queue id>: message-id=<id>` line gives the queue ID; the first `<queue id>: to=<...>, ... status=<status>` line from a delivery agent (smtp's, or error's while Postfix holds the relay unreachable) is the answer. Only `status=sent` accepts.
- A mail the relay defers or bounces, or answers nothing for within the 5 minutes, accepts nothing and fails the run. The next mail names the previous one and says what Postfix logged for it, so its changes are reported again.
