# tailscalerouter: the VCN's resolver moves to Unbound, with arawi's RPZ (2026-11-02)

- The VCN resolved `mail.regulad.xyz` to mail's public addresses. Instances that reach mail's public IPv4 arrive from their own public IPv4, outside the VCN ranges in docker-mailserver's fail2ban `ignoreip`. Failed relay logins on 2026-10-29, while the accounts were set up, got bailout (`129.213.123.169`) and mail itself (`132.145.154.200`) banned by the `postfix` jail for a week (6 failures within 604800 s). Over IPv6 they arrive from the VCN's /64, which `ignoreip` covers. Both bans were lifted by hand.
- arawi's split-horizon RPZ already maps `mail.regulad.xyz` to `mail.pubsub.wahlecloud.oraclevcn.com`, and tags the VCN as `lan`. dnsmasq can't serve that CNAME: its `cname=` target must be a record dnsmasq holds itself, not one from an upstream server.
- tailscalerouter now runs Unbound 1.24 from drop-ins in `/etc/unbound/conf.d/`, which EL's `unbound.conf` includes at the top level, after its own `server:` block.
  - **homelab-listen:** the addresses it listens on, from state, and who may ask.
  - **homelab-forward:** dnsmasq's forwarders, zone for zone, with `unblock-lan-zones` and `insecure-lan-zones` for the private reverse zones and `domain-insecure` for the internal ones.
  - **homelab-split-horizon:** `module-config: "respip validator iterator"`, the RPZ for every client, and arawi's uncached stub for `regulad.xyz`.
  - The RPZ is arawi's own file, through a symlink in `files/shared`, since a pfSense host's files stay in its own directory.
- EL's unbound package also ships `conf.d/unbound-local-root.conf`, an `auth-zone "."` fetched from the root servers with `for-upstream: yes`, beside our `forward-zone "."` to OCI's resolver. Public names resolve, and DNSSEC fails `dnssec-failed.org`.
- OCI's resolver gives instance names such as `mail.pubsub.wahlecloud.oraclevcn.com` an A record alone. Through the CNAME, `mail.regulad.xyz` and `livekit.regulad.xyz` resolve to private IPv4 alone, from the VCN as from the LAN.
- Switching the resolver:
  - tailscalerouter resolves through itself, so its packages have to be installed while dnsmasq runs.
  - `host_files` installs packages after writing files, and its dnf task refreshed `ol9_ksplice`'s metadata despite a `dnf makecache` minutes before. With dnsmasq stopped, that refresh failed, and the first attempt rolled back to dnsmasq after 1 min 44 s without DNS.
  - What worked: the drop-ins were already on disk, so `unbound-checkconf` passed them while dnsmasq still answered. Then one command stopped dnsmasq and started Unbound, with dnsmasq restarted if Unbound failed, and the apply converged afterwards.
- From tailscalerouter, a DNS query to arawi's ULA leaves over the tailnet by the `regulad-internal` subnet router's `fd83:b84c:aa57:1::/64` route and gets no answer, while IPv4 to `10.23.0.254` answers. The forwarders to arawi are IPv4 until that's fixed; it's in TODO.md.
