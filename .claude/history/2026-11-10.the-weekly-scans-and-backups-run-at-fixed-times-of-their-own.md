# The weekly scans and backups run at fixed times of their own (2026-11-10)

- clamav-scan, every `restic_jobs` job and ingress's caddy-build had `RandomizedDelaySec=` of their period with `FixedRandomDelay=true` and `Persistent=true`, the setting aide-report's timer left on 11-09: systemd counts the delay of a run missed while the host was down from the boot (systemd#21166), so a reboot shortly before a host's time put its weekly run off by up to a week.
- **clamav-scan** runs at `clamav_scan_minute`, a minute of the week from Monday's midnight in UTC: the first 8 hex digits of the SHA-1 of `<inventory_hostname> clamav-scan`, modulo 10080. The timer's name is in the seed because the bare host name, aide's seed, would give each host's scan its daily AIDE report's time of day (`h % 10080 % 1440 == h % 1440`).
- **restic jobs** run at the same hash of `<inventory_hostname> restic-<job>`, modulo the period's minutes (`restic_job_periods`: 1440 daily, 10080 weekly), with the weekday in `OnCalendar=` for a weekly job: `Mon *-*-* 10:27:00 UTC`. `systemd-analyze calendar` accepts every rendered time.
- **caddy-build**, a byte copy under `files/`, kept the slot its fixed random delay had given it, read from the timer's `LastTriggerUSec` and `NextElapseUSecRealtime`: `Tue *-*-* 05:06:00 UTC`, just after midnight in ingress's America/New_York, so its next run stayed on 11-17.
- **A daemon reload alone runs a timer whose calendar time moved past its last trigger.** The `restic` role reloads systemd but never restarts its timers, and prodingus's `restic-cephfs` and `restic-rbd` started at once, as `clamav-scan` did on its restart handler; `restic-simple`, whose new time came after its last run, did not. The rollout so started, together, each scan and backup whose new time had passed since its last run.
- `tests/test_playbooks.py`'s `Timers` fails on any `.timer` or `.timer.j2` in the repository with `Persistent=` and a `RandomizedDelaySec=` or `FixedRandomDelay=`.
- doppelgangerhq.com, stopped since 11-07, was not applied to and keeps the old scan timer until the role next runs there.
