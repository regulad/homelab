# aameter retired; its FreeIPA mail account and thylacine tunnel key removed (2026-11-01)

- Removing a guest's file and its `ha.tf` entry is enough to destroy it: `prevent_destroy` goes with the block. The provider destroyed the `ansible_host` and the HA resource first, then stopped and destroyed the running container in 10 s, and changed the `amd64v3` HA rule in place.
- A retired host's `null-` account is deleted with `maintenance.yml --tags mail-account-remove -e mail_relay_remove_account=null-<name>`, from either stage. It runs on `localhost` and delegates the FreeIPA call to the stage's `mail_relay_freeipa_delegate` (wako in `ansible-generated`, prodingus in `ansible-hardware`); implicit `localhost` reads the stage's `group_vars/all`, SOPS included. Delegating to wako warns `Found variable using reserved name 'name'`, because wako has a `name` host variable equal to its inventory name.
- thylacine is Darwin 21.6.0 (iOS), reached as `mobile@thylacine.tail11540.ts.net`, with its keys in `/var/mobile/.ssh/authorized_keys`. The tunnel's key was limited there with `restrict,port-forwarding,permitopen="127.0.0.1:8181",permitlisten="127.0.0.1:1",command="/usr/bin/false"`: `permitlisten` pinned `-R` to an unused port, since `authorized_keys` cannot disable remote forwarding alone. Only Parker's key remains.
