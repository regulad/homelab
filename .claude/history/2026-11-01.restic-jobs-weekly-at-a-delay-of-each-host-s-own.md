# restic: jobs weekly, at a delay of each host's own (2026-11-01)

- Each `restic_jobs` job sets `period` (`daily` or `weekly`) in place of `on_calendar`. Its timer gets `RandomizedDelaySec=` of the period's length and `FixedRandomDelay=true`. Every job now runs weekly; the host backups were daily before.
- A changed timer file takes effect on the role's `daemon-reload` alone. The running timers showed their new next runs without being restarted, and prodingus's `restic-simple`, due at 00:15 under the daily schedule, did not fire.
- For one timer, the weekly delay landed at the same time of day as the daily one: `restic-simple` at 00:15:30, dodo's `restic-snapshot` at 10:41:28, aboriginal's at 02:59:47. This fits a delay of the timer's hash modulo `RandomizedDelaySec=`.
- The delays put three jobs on Wednesday afternoon: prodingus's `restic-cephfs` at 16:01, aboriginal's `restic-forget` at 17:52, and prodingus's `restic-rbd` at 19:22. The last runs took about 1 minute (cephfs, incremental), 3.5 minutes (forget and prune) and 25 minutes (rbd), so none overlaps, within the prune's one-hour lock wait.
- aboriginal's and dodo's Proxmox descriptions no longer announce backup times. Dropping dodo's `description` argument plans as `-> null`, and Proxmox then holds no description for the VM.
