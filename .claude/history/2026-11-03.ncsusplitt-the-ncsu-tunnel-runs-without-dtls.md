# ncsusplitt: the NCSU tunnel runs without DTLS (2026-11-03)

- The symptom. Through the tailnet, `https://shib.ncsu.edu/` never loaded, while it did with Tailscale off. NC State's internal view, which the tailnet's split DNS reaches, answers `fvip-shib.oit.ncsu.edu`, `152.1.12.171`, inside ncsusplitt's routes; the public view answers an AWS load balancer. TCP connected, then the browser's ClientHello, about 1.5 KB with the ML-KEM key share, went out as a 1240-byte segment that was never acknowledged.
- NC State's gateway dropped every DTLS datagram from ncsusplitt over 1200 bytes, an inner packet over 1134, with no ICMP error, while setting a tunnel MTU of 1399. The same gateway took 1328-byte ICMP, NC State's `ed11.ddi.ncsu.edu` answered 1400-byte UDP over the internet, and DTLS datagrams of 1456 bytes arrived from the gateway, so only DTLS toward the gateway was limited. DNS, pings and an X25519-only ClientHello fit, which hid it; curl in WSL on winlite also got through, as its segments reached ncsusplitt at about 800 bytes.
- openconnect sizes tun0 at the larger of the gateway's `X-CSTP-MTU` and `X-DTLS-MTU` (`cstp.c`); `--mtu` and `--base-mtu` only change what it requests, and `--base-mtu` is raised to at least 1280. It advertises `X-CSTP-Base-MTU` from the CSTP socket's `tcpi_pmtu`, up to eth0's 9000. Its DTLS MTU probe after each DTLS handshake (`dtls.c`, `probe_mtu`) gives up on any read error: after a DTLS reconnect, a 1400-byte hop's fragmentation-needed reached the connected socket as `EMSGSIZE` ("Failed to recv DPD request (-5)"), and the MTU stayed 1399. After the first connect on 2026-11-01 the probe reported no change. vpn-slice ignores `reconnect`, so tun0's MTU is set only at the first connect.
- With `--no-dtls`, everything goes over the CSTP channel: 1399-byte inner packets, 1280-byte packets from the tailnet and a default ClientHello all pass. Unit changes do not restart `ncsu-tun`, so the flag took effect at a restart, one login with a TOTP code.
- Unbound on ncsusplitt has listened only on eth0's and tailscale0's link-local addresses since the 2026-11-01 boot: eth0's DHCPv4 lease came at 19:07:29, after the drop-in's minute-long wait for tailscale0's address ended at 19:04:14.
