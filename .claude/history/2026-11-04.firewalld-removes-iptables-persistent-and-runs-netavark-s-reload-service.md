# firewalld removes iptables-persistent and runs Netavark's reload service (2026-11-04)

- Netavark adds each Podman network's subnets to the trusted zone at run time only, never to the permanent zone. On mail, these were `mailserver_default`'s `10.89.0.0/24` and `fd8a:ced2:727b:8489::/64`. Any reload or restart of firewalld drops them.
- `netavark-firewalld-reload.service` (`PartOf=` and `WantedBy=firewalld.service`) applies Netavark's rules again when it starts, which is after firewalld, so that `systemctl restart firewalld` keeps working, and at each `Reloaded` signal. On mail it started 31 s before the apply reloaded firewalld, and the entries survived the reload.
- `oci-linux-config` restarts firewalld on upgrade when it is active (Orabug 35681144). The Oracle Linux instances can therefore reload firewalld without Ansible.
- Netavark 1.17.2 (mail) and 1.16.1 (aboriginal) ship the unit; aboriginal already had it enabled. dodo's Netavark 1.4.0 does not ship it, so a firewalld reload there still drops its networks until a container restarts or Netavark is upgraded.
- Before this change, mail's firewalld had not reloaded in at least 30 days. The `reloads` of its policy and `direct.xml` had never fired since their adoption.
- `iptables-persistent` and `netfilter-persistent` were on doppelgangerhq.com and vkasm, inert on both. The role removes them without purging, so on vkasm their configuration stays in dpkg's `rc` state. doppelgangerhq.com's were purged by hand.
