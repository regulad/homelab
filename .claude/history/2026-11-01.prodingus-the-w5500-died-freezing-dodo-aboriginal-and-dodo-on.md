# prodingus: the W5500 died, freezing dodo; aboriginal and dodo on software virtio (2026-11-01)

- At about 07:45 EST, prodingus's Radeon Pro W5500 (`0f:00.0`, Navi 14, behind its own switch `0d:00.0`/`0e:00.0` on root port `00:03.1`, 16 GT/s x8 at every boot) dropped off the bus. The root port showed the link down (`DLActive-`, `SltSta: Changed: LinkState+`), and every function of the card read `ffff`. `00:03.1` has no AER capability, so nothing recorded why. It was the first such drop in the eight boots the journal held, back to 2026-10-06.
- Ruled out as triggers: that morning's unattended-upgrades run (`libnss3` alone); load (dodo at about 0.7% CPU, the host's load about 2); runtime power management (the card never runtime-suspended that boot, as the display on DP-1 kept it active); ASPM (off on every link of the card). The lane errors latched on `00:03.1` (lanes 2, 4, 5) single nothing out: five ports with healthy links had them too. Unrecorded: temperatures and power draw (sysstat collects nothing on prodingus), and power events (aboriginal's `nut-monitor` is masked, so the UPS's transitions are logged nowhere).
- The card is dead: it did not enumerate after a reboot, nor after a power-off to S5, and its display output is garbage. The firmware then hides root port `00:03.1`, which shifts the IOMMU groups of the devices after it down by one: aboriginal's AX200 from 29 to 28 and its RX6800 from 27 to 26, so Proxmox refused to start aboriginal ("PCI device mapping invalid … 'iommugroup' does not match"). The PCI paths before the slot do not move.
- The first kernel line was the card's HDMI audio, `snd_hda_intel 0000:0f:00.1`, failing to leave D3hot, then the gfx ring's 10-second job timeout, `device lost from bus!`, and `GPU Recovery Failed: -19`. The `gfxoff` and SMU errors around them follow from the lost link, as in [SteamOS #2953](https://github.com/ValveSoftware/SteamOS/issues/2953).
- dodo rendered `virtio-gl` on that card. Its QEMU's main thread blocked in an amdgpu `drm_syncobj` wait while holding QEMU's main lock: QMP and the guest agent stopped answering, the guest left the network, and Proxmox still reported it running. Proxmox's i6300ESB is emulated on that main loop, so dodo's watchdog never fired. aboriginal also held the card's render node through `virtio-gl` and kept running.
- No in-place reset is possible once a guest has GPU memory. A killed QEMU stays in D state in `amdgpu_gart_unbind`, whose TLB flush waits on a fence the lost card never signals; the job's timeout ten seconds later fails recovery without signalling it. `amdgpu_gart_unbind` holds a `drm_dev_enter()` section, and amdgpu's removal of the device starts with `drm_dev_unplug()`'s `synchronize_srcu()`, which waits for it, before it force-completes the fences. Unplugging an eGPU hangs the same way ([SteamOS #3002](https://github.com/ValveSoftware/SteamOS/issues/3002)).
- Killing such a QEMU panics the host: the kdump role sets `kernel.hung_task_panic=1`. dodo's QEMU, killed at 14:10, and two of TTM's `ttm_bo_delayed_delete` workers sat in D state on the lost card's fences, and the hung-task check panicked prodingus at 14:14:42. kdump took until 14:53 to write its xz dump, 9.2 GB of prodingus's memory, before rebooting.
- A clean poweroff of prodingus hung for 30 minutes in `pve-ha-lrm`'s stop: with `ha_shutdown_policy = "migrate"` (`tofu/gonkputer`'s `cluster.tf`), the LRM goes into maintenance and waits for its HA services to migrate, and a single node has nowhere to send them. `poweroff.target`'s `JobTimeoutSec=30min` then forced it (`poweroff-force`), killing the guests still running.
- A host shutdown otherwise stops each guest with force-stop on by default (`stopall`, 180 s each), and `_do_vm_stop` sends SIGTERM and then SIGKILL as soon as QMP fails, so a wedged QEMU does not hold it up; one in D state outlives both.
- prodingus can be powered on remotely after a power-off: `rtcwake -u -m no -s <seconds>` arms the RTC (which keeps UTC) before `systemctl poweroff`, and `eth0` (the onboard I211, `3c:7c:3f:22:33:b3`) has magic-packet Wake-on-LAN, which overture or the JetKVM can send. Once, after the forced poweroff, it came back before the RTC alarm, from an unknown source; neither path has been shown to work alone. The JetKVM's ATX extension can only reset.
- aboriginal and dodo moved to software-rendered `virtio`, and the two mappings to the shifted groups, each with a FIXME until the card is replaced. procontainer (106), dormant since 2026-10-23, was deleted with its root disk.
