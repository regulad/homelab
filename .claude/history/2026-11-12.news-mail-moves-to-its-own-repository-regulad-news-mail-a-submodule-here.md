# News Mail moves to its own repository, regulad/news_mail, a submodule here (2026-11-12)

- Nextcloud constrains an app's ID, not its repository's name: the ID is lowercase ASCII letters, digits and underscores (`[a-z]+[a-z0-9_]*[a-z0-9]+` in the App Store's `info.xsd`), and must name the single top folder of a release archive. Of the App Store's apps for Nextcloud 35, 120 of the 130 in the `nextcloud` organisation live in a repository named exactly their ID, as do 188 of the 419 others; a `nextcloud-` or `nc-` prefix is next, at 78.
- For apps targeting Nextcloud 31 or later, `<licence>` takes an SPDX identifier, of which the developer manual lists `AGPL-3.0-only`, `AGPL-3.0-or-later`, `Apache-2.0`, `GPL-3.0-only`, `GPL-3.0-or-later`, `MIT` and `MPL-2.0`; the short names (`agpl`) are for 30 and older. All 130 of Nextcloud's own apps there are AGPL, and 392 of the 419 others. Nextcloud's newer repositories follow REUSE: `LICENSES/`, `REUSE.toml` and an SPDX header in each file; `app_template` declares `AGPL-3.0-or-later`.
- `info.xsd` fixes the order of `info.xml`'s elements, such as `website`, then `bugs`, then `repository`, before `dependencies`; `xmllint --noout --schema info.xsd` checks a manifest against it.
- The Probot Settings app applied a new repository's `.github/settings.yml`, its topics and features, within a minute of the first push.
- `community.general.filetree` lists dotfiles, a submodule's `.git` file among them; it gives `src` for files only, and its `root` has no trailing slash.
- `git submodule deinit` refuses a submodule whose addition is still only staged.
