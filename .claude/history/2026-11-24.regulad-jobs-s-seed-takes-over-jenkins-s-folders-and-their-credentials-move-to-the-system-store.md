# regulad/jobs's seed takes over Jenkins's folders, and their credentials move to the system store (2026-11-24)

- **Removing a `jobs:` script leaves its items.** Job DSL's `SeedJobConfigurator` runs each of Configuration as Code's `jobs:` scripts through `JenkinsDslScriptLoader` with a `JenkinsJobManagement`, and records nothing it generates; only a seed job's build step deletes items, by its `removedJobAction`. A folder dropped from this file stays on Jenkins, with its jobs and its own credentials, until a seed takes it over: after the 2026-11-24 apply and restart, Jenkins's API still listed `z4yx` with `pam_rssh` and `mcMMO-Dev` with `mcMMO`, and `z4yx`'s own store still held its `regulad-jenkins` and `svc-pam-rssh-ci`, which take precedence over the system store's inside it.
- **A seed rewrites the folders it defines,** so credentials kept in such a folder's store are lost at its next run; they live in the system store instead, where every item can use them.
- **`gitHubApp` in Configuration as Code** takes the same access settings as Job DSL's `gitHubAppCredentials`: `repositoryAccessStrategy: {specificRepositories: {owner, repositories}}` and `defaultPermissionsStrategy: CONTENTS_READ`, as github-branch-source's own JCasC fixture, `github-app-jcasc-minimal.yaml`, writes them.
