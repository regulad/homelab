# dodo's crash dumps complete, with the VM's watchdog kept alive in the crash kernel (2026-11-20)

- **The VM's watchdog reset dodo 60 s after each crash, before its dump was written.** The `watchdog` daemon arms QEMU's i6300esb (`action=reset`) at a 30 s heartbeat, whose second stage resets the VM 60 s after the last ping. The crash kernel boots only `kdump-tools-dump.service` (`systemd.unit=` on its command line), so neither the daemon nor the driver is there: Ubuntu keeps watchdog drivers from udev, and only the package's units load `watchdog_module`. Each test left a `dump-incomplete.xz`, the first truncated (`xz -l`: "Compressed data is corrupt"), and the crash kernel's journal ends at `running makedumpfile`, as its last minute is never synced.
- **Nothing showed on the console when the VM reset**, so the panic sysctls the `kdump` role sets, which would print, were not the cause, and EFI pstore recorded nothing. `echo "screendump /tmp/<n>.png -f png" | qm monitor <vmid>` on the node, about once a second through the crash, caught the screen up to the reset.
- **With `wd_keepalive.service` pulled into the dump unit**, it loaded the driver and pinged the device four seconds into the crash kernel's boot. dodo, with 32 GiB, a 1 GiB crash kernel on one CPU, and its root on LUKS, which the crash kernel's initramfs unlocked through clevis, wrote a 182 MB `dump.<stamp>.xz` in 89 s and was back in 111 s. kdump-tools then purged the two incomplete dumps, as `KDUMP_NUM_DUMPS` is 1.
