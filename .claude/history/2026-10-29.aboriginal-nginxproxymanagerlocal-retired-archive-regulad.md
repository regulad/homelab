# aboriginal: nginxproxymanagerlocal retired; archive.regulad.xyz on Caddy behind ldap-auth (2026-10-29)

- Every NPMplus proxy host was reached only through aboriginal's deleted tunnel: it listened on loopback for 80 and 443. `archive` (regulad's `dcef`, published by rootless Podman on both families) was its one host still wanted, guarded by HTTP basic auth through ldap-auth, bound to the freeipa stand-in as `svc-nginxproxymanager` and admitting `archiveusers` (9 members).
- On ingress, LinuxServer's `ldap-auth` is a Quadlet published on `127.0.0.1:8888` only, and Caddy's `forward_auth` asks it, setting every `X-Ldap-*` header and `X-CookieName` itself so a client cannot set them. Without a login it answers 401 with `WWW-Authenticate: Basic realm="archive"`, which Caddy hands to the browser; with one it binds to wako as `svc-ingress` over LDAPS, trusting the CA bundle bound from the host, and checks the filter.
- Caddy's unit rebuilds Caddy with xcaddy at every start, so a new environment variable would mean a restart and a rebuild. The bind password is a root-only file read through `{file./etc/caddy/ldap-bind-password}` instead: `caddy adapt` keeps the placeholder in the loaded configuration, so it is read per request, and a reload is enough.
- `svc-ingress`, like the other `svc-` accounts, is "Service LDAPBind" with its password expiring in 2038; `svc-ansible` can create and delete such accounts through `community.general.ipa_user`. `mail_relay_freeipa_host` and `_user` are that role's defaults, undefined outside it.
- After `/reload-plugins`, and in a session started after it, `PATH` can list alock 0.1.0's `bin` beside 0.2.0's, or 0.2.0's twice. 0.2.0's shim, resolving its targets with `--list-hosts`, then found a shim again in place of the real `ansible-playbook`, and spun without writing anything or taking a lock. One 0.2.0 entry, first, and no other, fixed it.
