# Guest firewalls for git, authentik, matrix and tvheadend, and UDP open for NAT traversal (2026-11-05)

- git, authentik and matrix (CTs 200 to 202) had `firewall=1` on their NICs but no `/etc/pve/firewall/<vmid>.fw`, so nothing filtered them; adding rules and options changed no NIC and restarted nothing. Turning tvheadend's NIC firewall back on made the provider reboot CT 127, which took 15 seconds this time.
- Nothing reaches the three directly but ingress's Caddy: Uptime Kuma checks each through its public URL, and git's SSH comes through the Cloudflare tunnel, whose cloudflared connects outbound and reaches Forgejo's 2222 on localhost. matrix's bridges listen on all addresses (mautrix-telegram 29317, mautrix-discord 29334), but Synapse reaches every bridge on localhost.
- tvheadend's clients are ingress, on the web UI's 9981, and aboriginal's Jellyfin, on HTSP's 9982 over aboriginal's GUA, which `dual_stack_internal_sources` admits.
- The cluster security group `udp`, a single rule with `proto = "udp"` and no source, destination or port, admits every UDP datagram of both families. It is on tvheadend, for its HDHomeRun tuner, git, for cloudflared, and uptime, tsexit, ncsusplitt and qb, for tailscaled's NAT traversal. Since it covers mDNS, these guests list no `mdns` group, and on qb it replaced the uTP and DHT rule and the rule for the CR1000B's SSDP answers. tailscalerouter (CT 122) and aboriginal run tailscaled too but bypass the firewall; prodingus and the OCI tailscalerouter are outside the Proxmox guest firewall.
- A connection ingress had opened from its GUA before git's firewall came up stayed established, as conntrack passes it; ingress's new connections come from its reserved IPv4 address.
