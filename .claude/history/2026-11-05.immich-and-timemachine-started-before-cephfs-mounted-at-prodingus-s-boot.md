# immich and timemachine started before CephFS mounted at prodingus's boot (2026-11-05)

- prodingus booted at 19:00 on 2026-11-01 and reached `pve-storage.target` at 19:01:52, as soon as its Ceph daemons had started. No MDS was active until about 19:03:30: pvestatd's attempts to mount `/mnt/pve/cephfs`, every one there was, failed with `no mds (Metadata Server) is up` until the mount succeeded at 19:03:42. Guest starts made no attempt of their own. `pve-ha-lrm` is ordered only after that target, and began starting HA guests at 19:02:06.
- A container mount point whose volume is a host path, such as immich's `mp0: /mnt/pve/cephfs,mp=/mnt/cephfs,shared=1`, names no storage, so Proxmox neither activates CephFS for it nor checks it before the start. immich (CT 116, started at 19:02:37) and timemachine (CT 104, at 19:02:06) got the empty directory on prodingus's root filesystem at `/mnt/cephfs`. CephFS, once mounted on the host, propagated into them over that bind (`shared,slave`), and `findmnt /mnt/cephfs` inside still lists both.
- immich: podman-compose created three of its four containers, failed `immich_server` at 19:03:29 with `statfs /mnt/cephfs/application/immich: no such file or directory`, and ran the other three. `podman-compose up` does not exit when one create fails, so `podman-compose@immich.service` stayed active, and neither its `Restart=` nor an Ansible run would bring the server back. It returned on 2026-11-05 at 00:11, when podman-auto-update pulled a new machine-learning image and restarted the unit: down three days and five hours.
- timemachine: its quadlet unit failed on the same `statfs` at 19:03:08 and, with `Restart=always` at systemd's default `RestartSec=100ms`, hit the start limit (`Start request repeated too quickly`) at 19:03:10. It stayed down until an Ansible run started it at 23:19.
- Exposed the same way, and late enough this time: qb (CT 210), whose mount source is a subdirectory of `/mnt/pve/cephfs` that exists only once CephFS is mounted, started from 19:03:26 to 19:03:49; arawi (VM 108), whose hookscript is on `cephfs:snippets`, at 19:03:49; tvheadend's service (CT 127) at 19:04:22; and aboriginal (VM 100), on the `cephfs` directory mapping through virtiofs, at 19:06:58 from `pve-guests`.
- Upstream, [Proxmox bug 4085](https://bugzilla.proxmox.com/show_bug.cgi?id=4085), "VMs with cicustom lack proper storage activation for CloudInit volume(s)", into which [4221](https://bugzilla.proxmox.com/show_bug.cgi?id=4221), "Start on Boot VMs with external CephFS storage attached start too soon", was merged, describes the same race at a whole cluster's boot. Its maintainer's position: HA cannot tell a transient start failure from a permanent one, so the node must make CephFS available before guests start, for example by hooking it into `pve-storage.target`; HA's start failure policy (`max_restart`) sets how many attempts a guest gets. Its patch activates the storage of cicustom volumes only, which a host-path mount point does not have; the bug stood at PATCH AVAILABLE when last changed, on 2024-01-25. In [forum thread 120116](https://forum.proxmox.com/threads/delaying-vms-until-ceph-cephfs-is-mounted.120116/), `After=pve-storage.target` on pvestatd is reported not to help, which fits the target being reached before an MDS is active.
- Left as is for now. The fix 4085 points to would be a oneshot unit on prodingus, wanted by and ordered before `pve-storage.target`, that waits within a bound for `/mnt/pve/cephfs` to be mounted by pvestatd; every HA guest, arawi and pihole included, would then wait for CephFS on a full boot, about 1 minute 50 seconds this time. A `RestartSec=` in the quadlet template would let `Restart=always` units outlast a failure of more than a second.
