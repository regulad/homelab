# Forgejo registration is open only to regulad.xyz addresses, through its sign-up form (2026-11-05)

- `[service] EMAIL_DOMAIN_ALLOWLIST = regulad.xyz`. In Forgejo 12.0.4 every account creation but an admin's passes `CreateUser`'s `ValidateEmail`, which checks it: the sign-up form, external-login registration, OAuth2 auto-registration and LDAP first sign-ins, and addresses added in settings or on the activation page. Admin-created users and admin edits are not checked.
- The list matches the lowercased domain exactly; subdomains need their own entry. With an allowlist set, `EMAIL_DOMAIN_BLOCKLIST` is ignored. With `[federation] ENABLED`, Forgejo appends `ROOT_URL`'s host, git.regulad.xyz, the domain of its federated users' `f<uuid>@…` placeholders; it has no MX record.
- `REGISTER_EMAIL_CONFIRM` gives the list its force: a self-registered account stays inactive until the mailed link is followed. LDAP-created accounts are active at once. The `freeipa` LDAP source creates users from `mail` (`<uid>@localhost.local` when empty), so a FreeIPA user needs a regulad.xyz address to get a Forgejo account.
- `[openid] ENABLE_OPENID_SIGNUP = false`: `/user/openid/register` answers 403, and an existing account can still sign in with OpenID.
- One account a stranger had registered (inactive, a qq.com address) was deleted with `forgejo admin user delete --id`.
- The CLI in the rootless image needs `--config /etc/gitea/app.ini`; the admin config page does not show the allowlist.
