# aporia-blue is retired, and the parked zones redirect to regulad.xyz through a Worker

- The Aporia Blue journal is retired: its Vercel project and its domain `aporia.blue` are destroyed, and `regulad/aporia-blue` is archived. The `redirect` Worker now answers the apex and `www` host of aporia.blue, nefumllc.com and regulad.net with a 301 to `https://regulad.xyz/`, whatever the path.
- Before this, `www.nefumllc.com` still served a Google Sites page of the dissolved LLC through a CNAME to `ghs.googlehosted.com`, and the other parked hosts served nothing. `nefumllc.com`'s `google-site-verification` TXT record stays.
- A Workers custom domain creates its hostname's DNS record itself, so the records it replaced (aporia.blue's A record to Vercel and its `_vercel` TXT, and `www.nefumllc.com`'s CNAME) were destroyed in one apply and the custom domains created in the next: OpenTofu orders nothing between an orphaned record's destroy and an unrelated resource's create.
- The custom domains served the redirect within seconds of the apply through 1.1.1.1. A resolver that had cached the hosts' earlier negative answers, or aporia.blue's old A record, went on returning them for a while; test through DNS over HTTPS (`curl --doh-url https://1.1.1.1/dns-query`) right after such a change.
