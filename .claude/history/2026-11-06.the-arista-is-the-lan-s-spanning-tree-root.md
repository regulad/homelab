# The Arista is the LAN's spanning-tree root (2026-11-06)

- On 2026-11-06, pihole (CT 112) stopped answering at 18:06 until Parker restarted it around 18:20. From 18:06:04 to 18:17:08, prodingus's vmbr0 logged its neighbor, the Arista (`8000.28:99:3a:fa:31:fa`), lost on eth0 and eth1 together, about every 45 s, each time followed by topology changes and port re-listening. Over the same span, arawi's dpinger alarmed LANGW and LAN_SLAAC, and its gateway groups failed over to the 5G GRE tunnel; the bailout gateways cleared at 18:20:47–50, and LANGW at 18:23:36. blocky recurses through arawi, so it failed with it, as did the tailnet path through tailscalerouter. The restart coincided with recovery rather than causing it: blocky's startup test at 18:19:58 still failed. What started the churn is undetermined.
- The root was the Verizon router (`7fff.1cf43f144bee`), behind the Arista's Ethernet49, with hello 2 s, max age 6 s and forward delay 2 s, tighter than 802.1D's `2 × (forward delay − 1) ≥ max age`. Every bridge takes the root's timers, and the Arista only relayed the router's BPDUs, so both of prodingus's ports losing it at once is what a gap in the router's BPDUs looks like.
- The Arista now originates them: priority 4096, max age 6 s, forward delay 4 s, hello 2 s, the shortest timers 802.1D allows with a 2 s hello. vmbr0 takes root `1000.28993afa31fa` with them; eth1 (Ethernet50) is its root port, and eth0 (Ethernet47) blocks. arawi logged no gateway alarm through the change.
- EOS takes `spanning-tree priority` only in multiples of 4096 (0–61440), `forward-time` 4–30 and `max-age` 6–40, and prints them in the order max-age, forward-time, priority.
- An `eos_config` check run killed mid-run left a pending configuration session (`ansible_179400872429`) holding a truncated configuration, which would have removed the interface settings from Ethernet6 on had it been committed. It was not, and the next run's own session left `show configuration sessions` empty.
- The SG300s keep their default priority, 32768; giving them 8192 was dropped. In check mode, that line sat after the header's `!`, before `port jumbo-frame`. A read-only `show spanning-tree` on switch45f304 through network_cli got no prompt within 120 s; whether it left a stalled session holding a slot was not checked.
