# clamav-scan: a weekly scan on every Linux host, verified on uptime (2026-11-01)

- The weekly scan is the `clamav` role's own walker, `files/clamav-scan`, not `clamdscan`. With `--move`, clamdscan 1.5.4 refuses a file over clamd's StreamMaxLength instead of scanning part of it, and clamdscan 1.4.3 does not check on the client side whether a path crosses filesystems or enters procfs.
- clamd refuses a whole INSTREAM that runs past StreamMaxLength, replying `INSTREAM size limit exceeded. ERROR` with no `stream:` prefix. clamav-scan therefore sends at most StreamMaxLength of each file and counts those files as scanned in their first StreamMaxLength alone. With a local clamd, as on doppelgangerhq.com, it passes the file's descriptor (`FILDES`) instead, so files are scanned whole.
- `RandomizedDelaySec=1w` with `FixedRandomDelay=true` gives each host the same delay every week, derived from its machine ID, the user and the timer's name. The scans spread over the week and stay put from cycle to cycle.
- For "another filesystem", mountinfo's major:minor identifies a superblock. `st_dev` can't, because btrfs gives each subvolume its own `st_dev` on one superblock. Bind mounts of the scanned filesystem are walked, and a directory reached twice is skipped by (`st_dev`, `st_ino`).
- On uptime, an EICAR file in `/tmp`, a tmpfs, was moved to `/root/quarantine` by copy and unlink, since a hard link cannot cross filesystems, and mailed to regulad@regulad.xyz. The run scanned 52,957 files, 3.2 GiB, in 12 minutes through the shared clamd, and skipped Podman's overlay and shm mounts under `/var` as other filesystems.
- In check mode, command tasks show as skipped. The podman role's `chattr +C` task changed dodo's `/var/lib/containers/storage` and its `overlay` in the apply, without having appeared as a change in the check.
- The full generated `site.yml` ran for about 50 minutes over 36 hosts (`forks = 10`). In two fleet-wide checks, the containers reached through `pct` failed at a looped task, `mail_relay : Postfix settings`, with "Error reading SSH protocol banner" or "Authentication timeout". A check of those containers alone passed. Every play has been tagged since, for tagged applies.
- After prodingus's 18:54 reboot, timemachine's Quadlet failed at 19:03: `statfs /mnt/cephfs/application/timemachine: no such file or directory`. When checked hours later, the container's `/mnt/cephfs` was two stacked mounts: the bind of the host's `/mnt/pve/cephfs`, resolving to the host's ext4 root, under a CephFS mount where the paths existed. The apply restarted the service.
