# mail renews by certbot's DNS-01, and its miner and log flood are gone (2026-11-04)

- mail had two renewal paths, and neither would have worked. Root's crontab ran `certbot/certbot renew` in a container twice a day, and `dms-certbot-renew.timer` ran `certbot/certbot:latest` with ports 80 and 443 published. The certificate's renewal file names the `dns-cloudflare` authenticator, which the plain `certbot/certbot` image lacks. Both are removed, with the `certbot/certbot` and `certbot/dns-cloudflare` images.
- EPEL's certbot 3.1.0, with `python3-certbot-dns-cloudflare`, renews through `certbot-renew.timer`, not `certbot.timer`. The `certbot` role takes the name from `certbot_timer`, set in `group_vars/rhel.yml`. On a host without certbot, a check run cannot start a timer whose package is still to be installed, so that task is skipped then, as the `firewalld` role does. `certbot renew --dry-run` against staging succeeded; dry runs skip deploy hooks.
- docker-mailserver's change detector, `changedetector` in its supervisord, runs only with file-based accounts, and mail's accounts are LDAP's, so it stays stopped and nothing reloads a renewed certificate. A deploy hook, `/etc/letsencrypt/renewal-hooks/deploy/mailserver`, runs `supervisorctl restart postfix dovecot` in the container.
- The same miner as on doppelgangerhq.com was there: `websocat-proxy.service` to an ngrok tunnel, `xmrig.service` as `miner`, and `/usr/local/bin/websocat`. Parker's own; removed with the user, its home and its mail spool.
- setroubleshoot-server logged every container access to the bind mounts under `/root`, labelled `admin_home_t`, while SELinux is permissive. With logrotate's `compress` commented out in the package's `logrotate.conf`, one rotated `/var/log/messages` reached 7.6 GB, and gzip took it to 441 MB in 2 min 43 s. setroubleshoot is removed and `compress` turned on. The root filesystem went from 89% to 64%.
- webhookd's binary in `/usr/local/bin` reported `Version: snapshot`, an unversioned build. upstream's `ncarlier/webhookd` v1.22.1 replaces it, pinned by the arm64 archive's SHA-256, which matches GitHub's published asset digest. Its TLS key and `.htpasswd` were mode 644, and `.htpasswd` was regulad's; both are now `webhookd`'s, mode 600. Its one hook restarts docker-mailserver when its monitor reports it down.
