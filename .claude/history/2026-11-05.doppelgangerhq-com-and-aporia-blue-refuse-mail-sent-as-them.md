# doppelgangerhq.com and aporia.blue refuse mail sent as them (2026-11-05)

- Neither published SPF, DMARC or MX, so anyone could send as them and receivers applied no policy. Each now has, per M3AAWG's 2022 parked-domains practice, a null MX (`0 .`, RFC 7505) and `v=spf1 -all` at the apex and at `*.`, and `_dmarc` `v=DMARC1; p=reject; sp=reject; adkim=s; aspf=s`. No DKIM record: M3AAWG advises none, and RFC 6376 fails a signature alike against a missing or an empty key. No `rua`: reports would count only mail that is rejected anyway.
- Nothing in the repository sends as either: `mail_relay` rewrites every sender to `null-<host>@regulad.xyz`, and doppelgangerhq.com's app has no SMTP settings. Supabase's Auth mail and OneSignal are configured outside it.
- Cloudflare takes `.` as MX content. It publishes `*._report._dmarc.dmarc-reports.cloudflare.net`, so any zone can report to DMARC Management once that is enabled, with no authorization record.
- The wildcard TXT answers `_dmarc.<sub>` and `<selector>._domainkey` too; DMARC and DKIM discard records without their version tag.
- 1.1.1.1 kept serving NXDOMAIN for `_dmarc.aporia.blue` and `*.aporia.blue` names for a while after the apply, from lookups before it; the zone's nameservers answered at once.
- `tofu/cloudflare` has a `moved.tf`; HIBP's record is `doppelgangerhq_com_txt_hibp`.
