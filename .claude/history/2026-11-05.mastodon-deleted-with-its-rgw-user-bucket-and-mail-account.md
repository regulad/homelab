# mastodon deleted, with its RGW user, bucket and mail account (2026-11-05)

- Destroying a container removes its `/etc/pve/firewall/<vmid>.fw` with it, so the provider's state-only delete of its firewall options leaves nothing behind there. Its vzdump archives on `cephfs:backup` stay, and are kept.
- The `ceph_rgw` role's `rgw_user` and `rgw_bucket` modules only create and converge: a user or bucket dropped from host_vars stays on the gateway. `radosgw-admin bucket rm --bucket=<name>` refuses a bucket that still holds objects unless given `--purge-objects`; `radosgw-admin user rm --uid=<uid>` follows. `mastodon-media` was empty.
- aboriginal's rclone `rgw-buckets` remote combines the buckets in `rgw_backup_buckets` into `/mnt/rgw`; a bucket leaves that list, and `mnt-rgw.service` restarts, before the bucket is removed.
- alock resolves a `maintenance.yml` run's targets from the stage's inventory, not from the plays the tags select. In `ansible-generated`, `--tags mail-account-remove` without `--limit` is refused while any inventory host was last applied from a branch HEAD lacks, and `--limit localhost` matches nothing, since the implicit localhost is not in the inventory. From `ansible-hardware` it runs, delegating to prodingus, and records the hardware hosts (prodingus, turk).
