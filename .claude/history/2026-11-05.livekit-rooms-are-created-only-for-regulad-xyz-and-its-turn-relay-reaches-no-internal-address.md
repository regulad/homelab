# LiveKit rooms are created only for regulad.xyz, and its TURN relay reaches no internal address (2026-11-05)

- lk-jwt-service v0.4.0 matches `LIVEKIT_FULL_ACCESS_HOMESERVERS` against the request's `matrix_server_name`, the homeserver's `server_name` (regulad.xyz), not its client hostname: the old `matrix.regulad.xyz` matched no one, and calls had worked only because LiveKit created rooms on join. With `room.auto_create` off, LiveKit 1.9.9 answers a join to a missing room with 404, before ICE, so other homeservers' users join only rooms regulad.xyz's users made.
- LiveKit 1.9.9's TURN password is derived from the API secret and the participant ID, checked without looking the participant up, and never expires: issued credentials last until the secret rotates.
- pion/turn 4.1.3 opens every relay socket as udp4 on 0.0.0.0 within the relay range, even for a TCP REQUESTED-TRANSPORT, and filters no peer unless given a PermissionHandler, which LiveKit does not set. The `livekit-relay` firewalld policy (HOST to ANY) rejects UDP from `livekit_relay_ports` to 0/8, 10/8, 100.64/10, 169.254/16, 172.16/12 and 192.168/16.
- firewalld 1.3.4's `filter_OUTPUT` accepts established flows and `oifname "lo"` before any policy, so an output policy cannot filter traffic to the host's own addresses. Policy names are capped at 18 characters.
- livekit's relay range overlapped the ephemeral range (32768–60999); its resolver is 10.131.80.239 and chrony polls 169.254.169.254, so a source-port match needs `net.ipv4.ip_local_reserved_ports`, which leaves explicit binds alone.
