# The OCI instances have no NFS client (2026-11-05)

- The Oracle Linux 9 images ship `nfs-utils`, which requires `rpcbind`, with `rpcbind.socket` and `nfs-client.target` enabled: port 111 was open on every address with only the portmapper registered, and nothing mounted or exported NFS. On livekit, LiveKit's TURN relay reaches the host's own addresses over loopback, which firewalld accepts before any policy. `host_files_removed_packages` (dnf, or apt with purge) now removes `nfs-utils` and `rpcbind` there, and `nfs-common` and `rpcbind` on Ubuntu; doppelgangerhq.com's were purged by hand on 2026-11-04.
- Nothing required `nfs-utils`; `libnfsidmap` and `sssd-nfs-idmap` stay, as `sssd-common` requires them. Ansible's `dnf` module sets `clean_requirements_on_remove` from `autoremove`, off by default, so the orphaned `gssproxy` stays installed; it runs until the next boot, on Unix sockets only.
- The packages' `%preun` scripts `disable --now` their own units, the `rpc_pipefs` mount among them. systemd's disable deletes a `.wants` directory it empties, which aide-report does not explain, so `remote-fs.target.wants` was reported on bailout, livekit and tailscalerouter; mail keeps it for `mnt-mailserver.mount`.
- `oci-linux-config`'s `nfs_client_id.service` writes `/etc/modprobe.d/nfsclient.conf` with curl and does not need nfs-utils.
- doppelgangerhq.com's purge on 2026-11-04 left `run-rpc_pipefs.mount` mounted until its next boot.
