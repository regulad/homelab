# peppercord and cordpepperer reach no internal address (2026-11-06)

- The public PepperCord bots connect wherever a Discord user asks (yt-dlp's generic extractor, Minecraft lookups). The cluster security group `internet-only`, listed by both guests, rejects their new outbound connections to 0/8, 10/8, 100.64/10, 169.254/16, 172.16/12, 192.168/16, fc00::/7 and OCI's pubsub /64. Before the rejects it admits only DNS to arawi, mail's 465 and clamd's 3310. The LAN's GUAs are left to the firewalls of the hosts holding them. Tested after the apply, from each guest and from inside each bot container: arawi:443, mail:25 and arawi's ULA were refused within milliseconds, while a control guest without the group reached all three; Discord, DNS, mail:465 and clamd:3310 connected.
- pve-firewall 6.0.7 accepts RELATED,ESTABLISHED in `PVEFW-FORWARD` before any guest chain, so replies to inbound checks need no OUT rule. Its `dhcp` and `ndp` options admit 68→67, 546→547 and ICMPv6 133, 135 and 136 at the top of the OUT chain. A security group's OUT rules run as `GROUP-<name>-OUT`.
- Uptime Kuma checks the bots inbound, over IPv4 to :8080/health (monitors 60 and 61), and has no push monitors. Its heartbeats are in the `heartbeat` table of its embedded MariaDB (`-S /app/data/run/mariadb.sock kuma`).
- Neither guest runs Tailscale. They reach the tailnet and OCI through tailscalerouter on eth0, where the node's firewall sees the traffic. arawi answers `mail.regulad.xyz` with mail's private 10.131.80.15. clamd has no static mapping.
- The bots' Podman networks are IPv4-only, and aardvark forwards the bot containers' queries to the compose file's Google resolvers.
