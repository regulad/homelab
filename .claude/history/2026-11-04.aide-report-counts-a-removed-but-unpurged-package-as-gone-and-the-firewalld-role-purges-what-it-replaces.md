# aide-report counts a removed but unpurged package as gone, and the firewalld role purges what it replaces (2026-11-04)

From vkasm's report after the firewalld role removed iptables-persistent and netfilter-persistent (1.0.20) on 2026-11-04.

- **`dpkg-query -W` lists a removed package until it is purged.** A removal without a purge leaves the package as config-files (`rc`), with its version, its conffiles and its `.list` and `postrm` records. aide-report counted that as installed, so nothing the removal took away was explained: vkasm's report held 28 removed paths and the two packages' shrunken `.list` records. aide-report now reads `${db:Status-Status}` and counts a package in config-files or not-installed as not installed. It vouches for nothing such a package leaves, as `dpkg --verify` checks none of it. Replaying vkasm's report explained 201 changes, up from 173.
- **A removal's `postrm` may change a conffile it leaves behind.** netfilter-persistent's runs `chmod -x /etc/init.d/netfilter-persistent`, and the file stays until a purge, so AIDE reports its mode.
- **The firewalld role purges the managers it replaces on Debian and Ubuntu,** through `apt` with `purge: true`. Ansible's apt module removes a package that is installed, or that still has files when `purge` is set (`apt.py`), so this also purges what earlier runs left as config-files, such as ufw's. On Enterprise Linux it goes through `dnf`: rpm removes a package's configuration files, keeping a changed one as `.rpmsave`.
- **firewalld writes its zone files itself.** `ansible.posix.firewalld` makes a permanent change over D-Bus, and firewalld then writes `/etc/firewalld/zones/<zone>.xml`, which the module does not name, so AIDE's refresh missed it: vkasm's `public.xml`, added at 02:31:55 by the role's run. The role's three zone tasks now declare `aide_writes: [/etc/firewalld/zones/*.xml]`, every zone's file, since the declaration does not take the loop's item.
- **vkasm's `/boot/grub/grub.cfg`** was rewritten at 01:01:55 by kernel_params' update-grub, four seconds after the role wrote `99-audit.cfg`. The handler's `aide_writes` for it came at 01:17 (85daeab), after that run.
