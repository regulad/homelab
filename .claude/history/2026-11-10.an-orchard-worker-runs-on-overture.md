# An Orchard worker runs on overture (2026-11-10)

- **The worker as Orchard documents it**: a root launchd daemon, `org.cirruslabs.orchard.worker`, started with `--user _orchard`. `ps` shows the worker as `_orchard` and `orchard localnetworkhelper` as root. With `--log-file`, everything goes to that file (`/var/log/orchard-worker/worker.log`); launchd's own stdout and stderr log stays empty. At start, the worker logs `registered worker overture` and `using WebSocket-based v2 RPC`, then syncs its VMs every 5 seconds.
- **The controller's view of overture**: `/v1/workers` reports `resources` of `org.cirruslabs.logical-cores` 8, `org.cirruslabs.memory-mib` 8192 and `org.cirruslabs.tart-vms` 2, along with `defaultMemory` 3072 and `defaultCPU` 4. Those two fields are camelCase, while `last_seen` and `machine_id` beside them are snake_case.
- **Service accounts through the API**: GET `/v1/service-accounts` returns every account with its token, so a role can compare tokens without keeping any state of its own. POST creates an account, honouring the token it's given. PUT `/v1/service-accounts/<name>` takes the name from the body.
- **macOS's DHCP lease**: at every start, the worker warns that the default lease is one day. Tart's FAQ shortens it to 600 seconds with `DHCPLeaseTimeSecs` in the `bootpd` dictionary of `/Library/Preferences/SystemConfiguration/com.apple.InternetSharing.default.plist`. A one-day lease exhausts the pool only at about 253 VMs a day. The `orchard_worker` role doesn't set it.
- **Time Machine**: `tmutil addexclusion` without `-p` is sticky, an attribute on the item itself that follows it if it moves. `tmutil isexcluded` prints `[Excluded]` once it's set.
