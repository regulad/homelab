# aboriginal moves onto LUKS through an interim rbd disk, and boots from its own SSD at a host reboot (2026-11-21)

- **With `reboot_after_update = false`, the provider hot-plugs a new disk without a restart.** bpg/proxmox 0.122.0 created `virtio1` on the running VM 100 with its options (`aio=io_uring`, `cache=none`, `backup`, `replicate`) in 2 s, and the guest saw `/dev/vdb` at once. The boot order stays pending in Proxmox until the VM next starts (`qm pending 100`: `new boot: order=virtio1;virtio0`). The provider warns that a reboot is required, but it reads the pending value, so a fresh plan is a no-op.
- **btrfs moved 138.6 GiB in 34 minutes** while aboriginal's services ran, slower than dodo's 66 GiB in ten minutes, using 11.5 minutes of CPU and 1 GiB of memory at peak. `@ssd`, mounted at `/mnt/ssd`, moved with the rest of the filesystem.
- **`grub-install` updated the firmware's `Ubuntu` entry in place:** `Boot0002` kept its number and place in the boot order, and now names the interim EFI system partition's PARTUUID, which the copy onto the SSD kept.
- **A kernel installed but not yet booted gets clevis too.** 7.0.0-44 waited for aboriginal's next boot. `update-initramfs -u -k all` and the `luks` role's handler, which runs kdump-tools' hook for each installed kernel, gave both kernels an initramfs and a crash initramfs with clevis, and aboriginal's first encrypted boot was on 7.0.0-44.
- **The SSD's tail held 5 MB of the old btrfs.** Past the image's 238 GiB, 5,030,557 bytes were nonzero, against dodo's 123 bytes of backup GPT. `blkdiscard --offset` warned that the drive holds a GPT but discarded the range, and the SK hynix SC311 reads zeros after TRIM (`hdparm -I`: "Deterministic read ZEROs after TRIM"), so the range then read as zeros. The copy ran at 212 MB/s, 20 minutes.
- **Proxmox's protection flag refuses to detach a drive, not only to delete one.** The detach's apply failed with "can't remove drive 'virtio1' - protection mode enabled". The flag went from VM 100, the only guest OpenTofu manages that had it, as `prevent_destroy` guards the guests already, and `qm set 100 --protection 0` cleared it live before the detach.
- **The failed detach still dropped `virtio1` from OpenTofu's state.** The provider reads only the disks its state holds, so the next plan no longer detached it and changed only the boot order. `qm set 100 --delete virtio1` detached it by hand, and took it out of the boot order too.
- **`started = false` kept the detach from starting VM 100.** prodingus answered again 10 minutes after its reboot, and VM 100, at startup order 1, started 5 minutes after that. The vTPM unsealed the key, as SSH answered within a minute of the start. A plan made while later guests still waited on their startup delays showed vkasm's `started` going from false to true; once every guest had started, the plan was a no-op. The first plan after the reboot also failed once to mint the provider's root ticket over SSH, having attempted no public key; a retry passed.
- **Deleting the interim image stopped partway.** `qm set 100 --delete unused0` left the image in rbd's trash in its removing state, with 5246 data objects and its header, and the config still listing `unused0`; Proxmox's retry then failed to open it. Such an image doesn't appear in `rbd trash ls --all`, but `rbd ls` lists it, and `rados -p rbd_ssd listomapkeys rbd_trash` gives its id. `rbd trash rm rbd_ssd/<id>` removed it, and the stale `unused0` line came out of `/etc/pve/qemu-server/100.conf` by hand.
