# mail's docker-mailserver looks up its users on wako (2026-11-05)

- docker-mailserver reached wako's directory through the old server's stand-in, `freeipa.regulad-verizon-livingston.internal`, which rewrites `dc=home,dc=arpa` to `dc=regulad,dc=internal`. Its `LDAP_SERVER_HOST`, `LDAP_SEARCH_BASE` and `LDAP_BIND_DN` now come from `freeipa_server`, the suffix derived from its domain as arawi, FreeRADIUS and the stand-in derive theirs. `svc-mail` binds with the password it had, which the migration carried over.
- docker-mailserver writes its LDAP settings into Postfix's five `ldap-*.cf` maps, `dovecot-ldap.conf.ext` and `saslauthd.conf` as it sets up a new container, from those three settings and the filters.
- Before the switch, docker-mailserver's own filters ran as `svc-mail` against both endpoints, and returned the same entries: compared by count and by a hash of their sorted values, without printing them. The image has no `ldapsearch`, but its curl speaks LDAP: `curl -K - 'ldaps://<host>/<base>?<attributes>?sub?<filter>'`, with `user = "<bind DN>:<password>"` in the config it reads, and `&` in the filter written `%26`. A filter without an `objectClass` also matches the entry's copy in FreeIPA's compat tree.
- wako resolves to its IPv4 address and a ULA, and the container reaches both, through the VCN's route to the homelab. glibc's default address selection ranks IPv4 above a ULA, so libldap connects over IPv4. Binds, and TLS verified by name, work over each family.
- The stand-in's slapd logs each connection's source, at `stats`: mail's arrive from its private address, 10.131.80.15. None have arrived since the switch.
- docker-mailserver runs its change detector, which reloads Postfix and Dovecot when a certificate changes, only with `ACCOUNT_PROVISIONER=FILE`. With LDAP, a certificate certbot renews reaches them only when the container is next created.
