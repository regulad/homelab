# aboriginal: Docker removed; wlan0's firewalld zone named by its NetworkManager connection (2026-10-31)

- Purging `docker.io`, `docker-buildx`, `docker-compose-v2` and `containerd` keeps `runc`, which podman and buildah depend on. Docker's firewalld zone `docker` and policy `docker-forwarding`, permanent files in `/etc/firewalld`, outlive the package, and deleting them takes a reload.
- On each reload firewalld takes the zone of an interface NetworkManager manages from its connection, ahead of the zone files. With `connection.zone` empty, the reload put wlan0 in the default `public` zone, although `trusted.xml` lists it. Netplan's `networkmanager.passthrough` with `connection.zone: trusted` names it, and it held across a reload.
- From ingress, IPv6 to wlan0's ULA got no answer in either zone, while eth0's answered; ingress reaches that address at the AX200's Wi-Fi MAC. IPv4 to wlan0's address arrives on eth0, which answers ARP for it.
