# aboriginal: Nextcloud up the ladder to 35 (2026-10-31)

- 32.0.15, 33.0.10, 34.0.5, 35.0.2, one run of `nextcloud-upgrade` each. 33 updated checksum, contacts, news, notes and webapppassword from the App Store; 35 runs on PHP 8.5.
- The first run to 33 upgraded cleanly, then rolled back at its own check. ansible-core 2.21's `regex_search` with a group raises `'NoneType' object has no attribute 'group'` where nothing matches, and its group argument must reach it as `\1`: inside `{{ }}` in a folded scalar Jinja keeps a literal's backslashes, so `'\\1'` arrives with two, while in an assert's `that` it works as written.
- On the way to 35, `occ upgrade` disabled bookmarks 16.2.7 as incompatible, then failed to fetch the App Store's listing, `https://garm3.nextcloud.com/api/v1/apps.json`, some 16 MB compressed and 38.7 MB as JSON: it gave up at its 120-second default with half of it received, so 17.0.1, which the App Store has for 35, never came. The download took two to three minutes from the host and the container alike. The entrypoint did not print its list of disabled apps, which the play's check had relied on. The timeout is the app setting `settings appstore-timeout`, which the play now raises to 600 before an upgrade, and its check compares the enabled apps before and after.
- bookmarks 17.0.1 was installed by hand, checked as Nextcloud checks an App Store install: its certificate chained to `resources/codesigning/root.crt` with CN `bookmarks`, and the App Store's RSA-SHA512 signature verified over the archive. It replaced the disabled 16.2.7 in the apps volume, and `occ app:enable` migrated it.
- After 35, `occ maintenance:repair --include-expensive` ran the mimetype migrations `setupchecks` had listed since 33, in seconds.
- Nextcloud's PHP is past PHP2Shell, CVE-2026-109619 (GHSA-6wmf-4rv5-p3rp, "RCE via stale global state") and CVE-2026-109618 (GHSA-w8hw-w9fx-j79w, "RCE via OOM corruption"), which PHP fixed in 8.2.35, 8.3.36, 8.4.27 and 8.5.12. The stock image builds PHP from php.net's tarball: 35's is 8.5.12, its `PHP_SHA256` php.net's own for `php-8.5.12.tar.xz`, and the CLI and Apache's `libphp.so` both report it. 32.0.15's image had 8.3.36, while 32.0.3's, as `nextcloud-ca`'s, had the vulnerable 8.3.29.
