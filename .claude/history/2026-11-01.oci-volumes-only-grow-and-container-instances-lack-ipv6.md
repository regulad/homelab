# OCI volumes only grow, and Container Instances lack IPv6 (2026-11-01)

Checked against Oracle's documentation and its price API.

- **Volumes only grow.** A block volume is 50 GB to 32 TB. A boot volume is at least its image's default, 46.6 GB for every image in `tofu/oracle`, and a custom size is at least 50 GB.
- **Replacing a boot volume** keeps the instance's OCID and addresses, but it is a fresh OS.
- **Always Free storage is 200 GB in all,** boot volumes included.
- **Container Instances** have no IPv6 on their VNICs and no `NET_ADMIN`, are replaced on an image change, and get an ephemeral public IP. That rules them out for bailout, tailscalerouter and livekit.
