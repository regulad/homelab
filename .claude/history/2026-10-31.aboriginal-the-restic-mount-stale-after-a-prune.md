# aboriginal: the restic mount stale after a prune, and an orphan holding it (2026-10-31)

- `restic mount` reads the repository's index once, at its start. Thursday's `restic-forget` pruned at 12:30, removing packs the running mount's index still named, and from 15:25 the mount logged `Load(<data/…>) failed: Key not found` for them, 604 times by Saturday. Parts of the `nas` archive failed through it (`/mnt/nas/dump`, `/mnt/nas/snippets`), and by Saturday evening both overlays, `/mnt/media` and `/mnt/nextcloud`, answered EIO, although their lower and upper directories each listed. A fresh restic listed the snapshot without errors, and a new mount served every path.
- Restarting `mnt-restic.service` failed. The restic serving `/mnt/restic` had started at 02:40 on Thursday, before the unit's start that succeeded at 02:43, from an attempt the helper had given up on. With `KillMode=process` the stop killed only the process in the PID file and left that one, and the new restic's `fusermount` found the mountpoint "Transport endpoint is not connected". `Requires=` carried the restart to the mounts, the stacks and smbd, which came straight back on the old mount.
- The recovery: stop `mnt-nas.mount` and `mnt-restic.service`, which takes down everything built on the mount; SIGTERM the old restic, which exited; lazily unmount what was left; start `mnt-restic.service`, ready in 10 s; then start the stacks and smbd, which pull in their mounts.
- The unit now kills its whole cgroup at a stop (`KillMode=control-group`), and the forget job's `mounts` stops the mount before the run and starts what that took down after it.
