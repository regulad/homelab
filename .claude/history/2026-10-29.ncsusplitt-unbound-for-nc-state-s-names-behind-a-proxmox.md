# ncsusplitt: Unbound for NC State's names, behind a Proxmox firewall (2026-10-29)

- An earlier Unbound, set up by hand on 2026-08-15, answered `dns.json5`'s split DNS for `ncsu.edu` and `ncstate.net` at ncsusplitt's Tailscale addresses until 2026-10-15, when split DNS moved to vrd1 and vrd2 so that nodes on NC State's own network resolve without accepting routes. Its hand-written files in `/etc/unbound/unbound.conf.d` outlived the package's purge on 2026-10-22; the new configuration is built on them, without their IP-range access control.
- 152.1.14.78 is rdd42.ddi.ncsu.edu, a third resolver answering as vrd1 and vrd2 do.
- `interface: eth0` and `interface: tailscale0` bind each address the interface has when Unbound starts, link-local ones with their scope, and never the wildcard, so Unbound does not answer on `tun0` or `lo`. An address that appears later, such as a new GUA prefix, needs a restart, and a drop-in waits at boot for tailscale0's.
- Serve-stale answers come from the in-memory cache, which a restart empties: a reboot, when the tunnel also needs a Duo push, leaves nothing stale to serve.
- The Proxmox firewall needs no rule for Tailscale: tailscaled's random UDP ports pass as replies to its own probes, and peers on the LAN still connect directly.
- A `tofu/gonkputer` apply records its commit for the root and for every host in its inventory. It is refused while any of those was last applied from a branch HEAD lacks, and once applied, no other worktree can apply to a gonkputer guest until the commit reaches master.
