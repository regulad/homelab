# ClamAV reports a corrupt xz stream as an allocation failure (2026-11-04)

- **aboriginal's weekly scan mailed 26 "Can't allocate memory ERROR" lines,** all from one crate in regulad's Cargo cache, `lzma-sys` 0.1.20. They were its `.crate` and the deliberately corrupt test files of the xz it bundles, `xz-5.2/tests/files/bad-*.xz` and `unsupported-*.xz`. The host was not short of memory.
- **Every xz decoder error becomes `CL_EMEM`.** `cli_scanxz` in `libclamav/scanners.c` returns `CL_EMEM` for any result but `XZ_RESULT_OK` or `XZ_STREAM_END`, so a corrupt stream reads as an allocation failure. Upstream: [Cisco-Talos/clamav#1892](https://github.com/Cisco-Talos/clamav/issues/1892), open since 2026-11-03. It also reports that the error stops the scan before the file and its siblings are matched, here the `.crate`'s other members; the crate's unpacked copy in `registry/src` is scanned file by file.
- The lines come back each week while the crate stays in the cache. Nothing excludes them.
