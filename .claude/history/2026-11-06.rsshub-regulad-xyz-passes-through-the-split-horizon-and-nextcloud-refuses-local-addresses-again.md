# rsshub.regulad.xyz passes through the split horizon, and Nextcloud refuses local addresses again (2026-11-06)

- Correcting 2026-11-05: anonymous requests can make Nextcloud fetch a URL. A POST to `/index.php/ocm/shares` or `/ocm/notifications` signed with an unknown keyId makes it GET `/.well-known/ocm` and `/ocm-provider` from that key's origin (`SignatureManager::confirmIncomingRequestSignature` → `OCMSignatoryManager::getRemoteSignatory`); with `allow_local_remote_servers` the origin may be a LAN address. The switch is off again, and `homelab.config.php` with its bind is gone.
- Nextcloud 35.0.2 has no per-host allowlist: the switch is all or nothing, or a per-request `allow_local_address` an app sets in code, which News 29.0.0 does not. With the switch off, `DnsPinMiddleware` refuses a name if any A or AAAA is local and pins all of them; neither Nextcloud nor News sets `CURLOPT_IPRESOLVE`, and the image's curl 8.14.1 tries IPv6 first whatever the order. A failed TLS handshake does not fall back to IPv4, so this waited for ingress's AAAA to be its own.
- `rsshub.regulad.xyz IN CNAME rpz-passthru.` in the split-horizon RPZ, on arawi and tailscalerouter, ends RPZ processing for the whole query, CNAME targets included: the worker sets the query's `rpz_passthru`, which `rpz_callback_from_iterator_cname` checks first (unbound 1.26.1, since unbound#628's fix, 2022). LAN and VCN clients get its public records, 173.54.15.27 and ingress's GUA; the Verizon router hairpins the WAN address's port 443 to ingress.
- A caching stub such as systemd-resolved keeps the public records of ingress.regulad.xyz that came in rsshub's CNAME chain, and answers a direct lookup of ingress.regulad.xyz with them until their TTL runs out; arawi itself still rewrites it.
- Nextcloud's `Config::writeData` saves its merged configuration, every `*.config.php` included, into `config.php` whenever a setting is saved: a key there is unset by removing it from its file, then `occ config:system:delete`. A file bound into a named volume leaves an empty mountpoint file there once the bind goes; host_files never deletes a file it no longer lists. Both were removed by hand.
