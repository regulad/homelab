# regulad/jobs's seed moves into the regulad folder as its own Jenkinsfile, and svc-jobs goes (2026-11-21)

- **An organization folder takes no hand-made items.** `regulad` is a computed folder: it holds only the multibranch projects it computes from regulad's repositories, and nothing can be created in it. A repository joins it by having a root `Jenkinsfile`; regulad/jobs's push of one made `regulad/jobs` within seconds, from the App's push event, without a scan.
- **Job DSL 3752's seed collision** (`failOnSeedCollision`) refuses an item only while the seed recorded for it still exists (`JenkinsJobManagement.createOrUpdateConfig`). Deleting a seed removes only its own entry from the generated-job map (`DescriptorImpl.GeneratedJobMapItemListener`), never the items it generated: `z4yx/pam_rssh` and `mcMMO-Dev/mcMMO` stayed, with their builds, after the root `jobs` was deleted, and the new seed's first build listed them as "Added items" and took them over in place.
- **`jobDsl` in a Jenkinsfile** loads its scripts as the freestyle build step does while `useScriptSecurity` is false (`JenkinsDslScriptLoader`, sandboxed or not), so the freestyle seed's settings carried over unchanged. `when { beforeAgent true; branch 'main' }` on a stage with its own agent keeps other branches' and pull requests' builds from starting one. The seed checks out through the `regulad` folder's `regulad-jenkins`, and the folder reports its result on the commit.
- **community.general 13.5.0's `ipa_group`** failed svc-jobs's removal from jenkinsusers, which had succeeded: the bug the jenkins role's FIXME names. The play stopped there, so `aide-refresh.yml` skipped the host and its check stayed held until a rerun, whose refresh consumed the failed run's record.
- **The jenkins role never deletes a FreeIPA user;** svc-jobs went by an ad hoc `community.general.ipa_user` with `state=absent`, its `ipa_pass` templated from inventory, so it was on no command line.
- **`sops unset` leaves the removed key's comment behind,** orphaned at the end of the file; `sops edit` with an `EDITOR` script that drops trailing comment lines, and prints nothing, removed it.
