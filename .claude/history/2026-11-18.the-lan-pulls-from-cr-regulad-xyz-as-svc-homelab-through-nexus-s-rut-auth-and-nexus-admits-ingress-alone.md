# The LAN pulls from cr.regulad.xyz as svc-homelab through Nexus's Rut Auth, and Nexus admits ingress alone (2026-11-18)

- **Registry credentials before this:** only doppelgangerhq.com had any. Its root `config.json` held `ghcr.io` as `regulad`, Parker's classic PAT, and `cr.regulad.xyz` as `svc-doppelganger`. aboriginal kept a 2025 `/home/regulad/.docker/config.json` with no logins in it. Every other container host pulled anonymously: the gonkputer containers, dodo (Wings' `registries: {}`), vkasm, mail and pihole2. Nexus's `ghcr-io` proxy logs in upstream as `regulad`, and its `docker-io` proxy, now on `https://registry-1.docker.io`, with nothing.
- **Rut Auth on Community Edition:** Nexus 3.97.3 Community offers `rutauth-realm` and a `/v1/capabilities` REST API. The capability's type is `rutauth`, with one property, `httpHeader`. Enabling it adds the realm to the active ones, and Sonatype's guide moves it to the top. `POST /v1/capabilities` answers 200, but `PUT /v1/security/realms/active` answers 204, though the OpenAPI document lists only 200 for both.
- **Rut Auth trusts any name:** it takes whichever user the header names, admins included. So every Caddy site in front of Nexus (repo, cr, ios) drops a client's copy, and Nexus's 8081 admits only ingress's 10.23.2.31. A forged `X-Homelab-Peer: svc-ansible`, sent from the LAN and from OCI, was logged as anonymous.
- **Nexus's request log:** Nexus also runs the `http.forwarded` capability, so `request.log` shows the client address Caddy forwards, not ingress, and the authenticated user in its third field.
- **The ping is answered too:** with the header, Nexus answers the LAN's `GET /v2/` with 200 instead of a challenge. A Docker or Podman client on the LAN therefore never presents credentials of its own to cr.regulad.xyz, and pulls as svc-homelab even when it holds a login. Requests that do carry `Authorization` keep their own user.
- **How the LAN arrives:** arawi's split-horizon RPZ resolves cr and repo.regulad.xyz to ingress's ULA and IPv4 alone, so LAN clients reach Caddy from their ULA or 10.23.x address, which `remote_ip` matches.
- **The firewall takes a moment:** a request made seconds after the firewall apply still reached 8081 directly; the next ones, from prodingus and from uptime, timed out over both families.
- **Uptime Kuma 2's monitors** are in its embedded MariaDB, not `kuma.db`: `podman exec uptime-kuma mariadb -S /app/data/run/mariadb.sock -u root kuma`. Its Nexus monitor is `https://repo.regulad.xyz`, through ingress.
- **A failed run leaves AIDE's hold on:** `aide-refresh.yml` skips a host whose play failed, so nexus's daily check stayed held after the realm task failed, until a run that completes there or `RUN_HOLD` (6 hours). A rerun with the fix released it.
